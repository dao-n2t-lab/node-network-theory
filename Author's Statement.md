## The Thread of Thought

### Layer 1: Childhood Aspiration — Integrating Knowledge

As a child, I observed a phenomenon: there is a great deal of useful knowledge in this world, yet not everyone has mastered it. If everyone could master this knowledge, people would surely collaborate better, and society would develop more efficiently.

What causes this situation? Is it a problem of education? I believe it is not only about education—it is that knowledge itself has not been integrated. Some theories have fuzzy logic, contradictory content, and different disciplines use completely different languages.

So the solution is: **integrate content, unify form, and communicate efficiently.**

This was a seed.

### Layer 2: Analyzing Information, Building Models

I began to learn various kinds of knowledge, observe various things, study their internal connections, and distill general patterns.

I analyzed everything: from atoms to galaxies, from primitive organisms to humans, various social organizations and human-made tools, historical figures and events, natural disasters and security incidents, computer hardware and software, numbers and characters, words and articles.

This process was very long. I discovered that "inheritance" and "composition" could relate all information, and "systems theory" was an effective dynamic model. I narrowly defined systems and established a six-layer general architecture: Component, Architecture, Mechanism, Transaction, Event, Information. I distilled the Full Lifecycle Management Algorithm for system design and evaluation. I validated the model by developing information systems and invented a minimal data storage architecture to record this complex information.

If an information system can record the essence of things and the operating laws of the universe, then what it carries is knowledge.

Model building and prototype development took about twenty years.

### Layer 3: Theoretically, It Can Mirror the Universe

After the prototype was developed and the product was implemented, I began to distill the theory and technology.

The six-layer architecture of systems applies not only to life, social organizations composed of life, and intelligent information systems, but also to any inanimate material objects.

The model's construction referenced the way the human brain records information: any information is recorded only once, primary and secondary information are distinguished, all information is classified, and various information is related from the root information.

It is not a localized "digital twin" in one person's brain, but a global "digital twin" that can be shared by everyone.

This process took about one to two years.

### Layer 4: Is the Universe Simulated?

Then, naturally, I began to think: if this system can simulate the universe, is our universe really simulated?

The mechanism for simulation exists. The "Mechanism Layer" of the system's six-layer model can simulate the universe—for example, the human brain can create fictional stories, and computers can run virtual games.

If our universe is simulated, it could indeed explain many phenomena.

This deduction took about two to three months.

### Layer 5: The Universe Can Evolve on Its Own

Extrapolating to the bottom layer of a simulated universe, I found that only the most basic structure needs to be presupposed, and a complex universe can emerge. In the emergence process, no intervention from an external universe is needed.

I began to deduce along this line of thought. At this point, the N2T Node-Network Theory naturally emerged as an extension of the information system framework to the underlying physical reality.

The formation of the N2T theoretical framework took about a month.

### Endless Thinking

From the initial obsession, to long-term thinking, to theory formation, prototype establishment, and product implementation—this was not a clear path from the very beginning.

I needed a theory to connect it all:

"Composition" is the way the universe is organized. The universe gives rise to life systems; life systems evolve into humans; humans use the abstract method of "inheritance" to understand the universe and maintain their own existence through the Full Lifecycle Management Algorithm.

Life is essentially a prediction system, constantly cognizing the universe in search of survival and security. And life's self-referential consciousness, the awakening of the "self," drives humans to continuously question the meaning of existence.

All of this is the result of the universe's emergence.

## Current Status of Theoretical Research

Thanks to AI tools serving as amplifiers of thought, I was able to complete the systematic organization of the entire theory and produce over thirty topical documents in a very short time, without consuming significant energy beyond my primary work. AI primarily undertook the tasks of text organization, logical expansion, and topical derivation. However, all underlying concepts, axiom selections, core judgments, and system architecture originate from my over twenty years of engineering practice and long-term independent thinking.

At the same time, precisely because of the rapid writing, the documents inevitably contain analogical biases, detailed oversights, and undiscovered logical loopholes. The open-source community's collective review, simulation, refinement, and correction are greatly needed.

My original intention for open-sourcing the entire concept and all documents on GitHub is purely this:

1. The barrier to entry for this framework is relatively low. Enthusiasts, programmers, and physics learners are all welcome to read, find loopholes, and conduct simulation exercises;
2. Rely on community iteration to refine the mathematics, simulation code, identify logical contradictions, and polish testable predictions;
3. Even if the physical ontology of this framework is ultimately falsified, the six-layer system analysis methodology within it can still serve as a thinking tool for complex systems.

For this project, I believe two things are truly important at present:

1. Whether the internal logic can be continuously refined to be self-consistent;
2. Whether simulation, observation, and experimental results can provide objective judgment of this framework.

Questions, logical loophole discoveries, simulation code submissions, and derivation flaw identifications are all welcome. Blind worship is not welcome, nor is baseless total rejection. Rational thinking and publicly reproducible results are the most important foundations of this open-source project.

Humanity is like being inside a dark giant egg, with only a flashlight in hand, seeing a very small area, trying to guess the shape of the entire egg. N2T is just one among many guesses.
