## I. Forces at the Node Level: The Sole Rule Driven by Phase Differences

At the bottom layer of the N2T framework, there is only one rule of interaction between two nodes:

- **In-phase** (phase difference is $0$ or an integer multiple of $2\pi$) $\rightarrow$ Attraction (tendency to synchronize vibrations);
- **Opposite-phase** (phase difference is an odd multiple of $\pi$) $\rightarrow$ Repulsion (tendency toward destructive interference);
- **Other phase differences** $\rightarrow$ Stress (tendency to drive phases toward a matching alignment).

This rule is the sole source of all forces.

In the N2T framework, the interaction between any two nodes is determined by the phase difference $\Delta \theta = \theta_i - \theta_j$:

$$F_{ij} \propto -\nabla S$$

where the total stress $S$ contains both phase stress and geometric stress terms:

$$S = \sum_{(i,j)} \left[ \alpha (\theta_i - \theta_j - \gamma_{ij})^2 + \beta (L_{ij} - L_0)^2 \right]$$

**Phase differences drive everything. Forces at different scales are merely macroscopic manifestations of this rule in different topological structures.**

## VIII. The Unified Stress Function

All four forces can be traced back to the two components of the same stress function:

$$S = \sum_{(i,j)} \left[ \alpha(\theta_i - \theta_j - \gamma_{ij})^2 + \beta(L_{ij} - L_0)^2 \right]$$

where:

- $\theta_i, \theta_j$: phases of the two nodes
- $\gamma_{ij}$: phase factor on the edge (inherent offset when connecting)
- $L_{ij}$: actual length of the edge
- $L_0$: natural length of the edge (baseline length when stress-free)
- $\alpha$: coupling coefficient—how much phase stress is generated by one unit of phase difference between two nodes
- $\beta$: coupling coefficient—how much geometric stress is generated by one unit of edge length deviation from $L_0$

The first term is **phase stress**, coming from phase differences between nodes, corresponding to electromagnetism, magnetism, and chemical bonds.

The second term is **geometric stress**, coming from edge length deviation from the natural length, corresponding to gravity, the strong force, and the weak force.

And the source of both terms is the phase differences between nodes—**phase differences drive everything.**

In the N2T framework, the four fundamental forces are not four independent interactions, but four macroscopic manifestations of the same stress function at different scales and topologies:

In the N2T framework, there are no "four fundamental forces"—there is only one force: **stress driven by phase differences. Everything else is its projection.**
