### Key Distinguishing Notes

1. **N2T uses only ordinary binary edges at the底层**; it does not introduce hyperedges. This is an ontological difference from Wolfram hypergraphs—do not confuse them.

2. **Causal set theory's fundamental unit is the event**; N2T's fundamental unit is the node (physical primitive), and events are only one layer in the six-layer model—the ontologies are completely different.

3. Wolfram's hypergraphs and causal set theory are both physical hypotheses and, like N2T, have not yet been experimentally confirmed.

All three belong to the discrete ontology approach, holding that spacetime is not the most fundamental container but a coarse-grained emergent product of underlying structures. Wolfram hypergraphs use computational rewriting rules to drive hypergraph evolution; causal set theory uses the causal partial order of events as the sole underlying constraint.

**The greatest difference between N2T and the other two lies in its origin and scope of applicability**: N2T starts from an information engineering metamodel and not only deduces the physical universe but also introduces the critical transition of self-replicating units and six-layer universal modeling, incorporating life, artificial intelligence, and social organizations into the same theoretical framework. This is a part not covered by the other two models.
