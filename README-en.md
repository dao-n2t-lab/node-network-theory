## Project Introduction

This project is a thought experiment on how the universe came into being. It proposes a minimalist physical model based on "nodes + edges" (Node Network Theory, N2T). Adopting very few parameters (number of nodes, node spacing, node frequency), the model attempts to deduce the universe.

The evolutionary process of the universe can be summarized as:  
Universe initialization → Formation of matter → Emergence of life → Understanding the universe → Building models → Simulating the universe

Human beings are currently in the evolutionary stage of "understanding the universe and building models".

As a senior IT engineer, the author’s original research goal was to build a system capable of managing all kinds of information. After nearly thirty years of research, the foundational theory of "information meta‑model + system meta‑model + full‑life‑cycle management algorithm" has been established, with prototype development and product implementation completed. Relying on two minimalist binary relationships — **inheritance** and **containment**, all kinds of human‑society information are associated to achieve "unique recording and comprehensive correlation" of information. It bears the potential to build a global digital twin for human society. The underlying technology is "information nodes + information relationships" (specific engineering technologies are outside the scope of this project).

After the development of this engineering model was finished, it was found that this paradigm could be extended to construct a cosmic model.  
This is an intellectual journey extending from engineering practice toward theoretical exploration.

## Central Idea

### 1. N2T Node‑Network Substrate

Taking "nodes + edges" as the fundamental underlying unit, it deduces the evolutionary path of the universe: node networks, quarks, elementary particles, atoms, molecules, stars, galaxies, neutron stars, black holes, the universe, and further extends to life and social systems.

### 2. Six‑Layer General Modeling Paradigm

Component Layer｜Architecture Layer｜Mechanism Layer｜Transaction Layer｜Event Layer｜Information Layer.

It can describe both physical objects in the universe and system objects with environmental recognition and active self‑sustaining capability, including life, artificial intelligence, enterprises, states and other complex organizations.

### 3. Transition via Self‑replicating Units

In cosmic evolution, the emergence of self‑replicating units serves as the critical threshold from mere physical objects to living systems. It covers chemical replicators, biological genetic codes, and man‑made computing systems.
